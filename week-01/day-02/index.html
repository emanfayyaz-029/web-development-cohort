About IEEE LGU Student Branch

IEEE Lahore Garrison University Student Branch provides a platform where students can learn, share ideas and participate in activities related to technology and engineering.

The branch encourages students to develop their technical knowledge, teamwork, communication and professional skills through different educational and technical activities.

Our Purpose

  1. Encourage technical learning and innovation.
  2. Provide opportunities for practical learning.
  3. Develop professional and communication skills.
  4. Connect students with technical communities.
